# Spec: Issue #12, the sea angel replaces the cursor koi (deep-ocean rebrand, steps 1-3)

## Goal

Port the approved sea angel prototype into the site in place of the cursor koi: shared steering, the angel, and the heart at `.koi-home`, with the prototype's look and behaviour unchanged.

## Decisions (approved)

- The prototype (`sea-angel-study.html`) is the source for shape, look, timing and steering. Where the plan doc differs, the prototype wins.
- Phones: the angel swims to where you tap and circles that spot until the next tap. It hovers only before the first tap. Scrolling never moves the target.
- Size: `s` (.15 of the smaller window side, clamped to 80-280 px). About 135 px at 1440 x 900.
- Reduced motion: one still angel at the last screen's heart spot, heart inside, scrolling with the page.
- One heart per visit. Once eaten it stays inside the angel in both modes.
- Merges into `rebrand/deep-ocean`, not `master`. The pale background stays until the background spec.

## Out of scope

- The background, theme, tokens, intro, lily pads, README and `og.jpg`.
- Deleting the koi, wake, paint, paper and lily code and CSS (step 6).
- A glow trail or wake: the prototype has none.

## Changes

- `src/lib/swim.ts` (new): `clamp`, `ease`, `wrap`, `steer(body, x, y, dt, tuning)`, `move(body, k)`. The prototype retuned the koi's constants, so `steer` takes them as parameters and both tunings stay exact. `CursorKoi.tsx` imports these and keeps its own tail and wake code.
- `src/lib/angel.ts` (new): the prototype without its background, labels and controls. State and `updateAngel` need no canvas. `AngelPainter` makes its sprites and halo canvas in its constructor, because the page is pre-rendered and nothing may touch `document` when the module loads. Two additions: `heart.shown` (no lure while the heart is off screen) and `input.tap` (a tap target never goes idle).
- `src/components/effects/CursorAngel.tsx` (new): one fixed, `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none` canvas, device pixel ratio capped at 2, time step capped at 50 ms. Runs once revealed. Mouse and pen follow `pointermove`; touch uses a `pointerup` within 12 px of its `pointerdown`. The heart sits at the centre of the showing `.koi-home` and freezes there when the bite starts. Sized from its CSS box, so a phone's toolbar does not rebuild it.
- `src/lib/htmlState.ts`: `EATEN_KEY`, `heartEaten()`, `markHeartEaten()` on `sessionStorage`, in try/catch. Written when the swallow finishes.
- `src/App.tsx`: `<CursorAngel />` in place of `<CursorKoi />`. `src/styles/page.css`: `.cursor-angel`. `src/components/ScreenFoot.tsx`: comment only.

## Tests (Vitest)

1. `src/lib/swim.test.ts`: `ease` is the same at 60 and 144 Hz; `wrap` across ±π; `steer` never exceeds its turn rate and circles inside `idle`; the koi tuning matches the old formula.
2. `src/lib/angel.test.ts`: hovers head-up with no pointer; chases, then circles; the mouse hovers after 1.6 s; a tap keeps circling after 10 s; no lure while the heart is hidden; the eat timeline ends carried at 1.45 s with a flutter; it never eats twice; every value stays finite.
3. `src/lib/htmlState.test.ts`: eaten round trip, and blocked storage does not throw.
4. `src/App.test.tsx` still passes, which proves the pre-render is safe.

Pass checks: `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm run format:check`, `npm run build`.

## Risks and open questions

- The halo is added light, but on a see-through canvas the browser blends it normally, so it may be slightly dimmer where the water is bright. Measure it, and show it before changing anything.
- It looks washed out on the pale background until the background spec. Expected.
- Frame time on a real phone is unmeasured.
- For later specs: what replaces the paper intro, and whether Contact's paper notes stay.
