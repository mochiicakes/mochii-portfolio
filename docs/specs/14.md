# Spec 14: the dark theme and liquid glass kit (deep-ocean rebrand, step 5)

The number is provisional until a GitHub issue exists.

## Goal

Give the page its dark theme and one small liquid glass kit, with layouts, content and behaviour unchanged.

## Decisions (approved)

- The kit follows the `liquid-glass-design` skill (affaan-m/ecc), installed in `.claude/skills/`.
- The skill is for Apple's SwiftUI, UIKit and WidgetKit and has no web code, so its rules are rebuilt in CSS. No package is added.
- Accents are icy blue. Body text is white. `--bleed` is removed. Orange and pink belong only to the angel and the heart.
- Hero buttons keep one shared frame size, and focus fills the frame instead of adding a second one.

## How the skill's rules map to this site

| Skill                                  | Here                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `.regular`, `.tint`, `.interactive()`  | `.glass`, `.glass-tint` (icy blue), `.glass-press` (reacts to hover and touch) |
| Capsule, rounded rect, circle          | Buttons, tabs and the mode switch; panes and cards; arrows and play buttons    |
| `.glass` and `.glassProminent` buttons | Every `.btn`; Get in touch is the prominent one                                |
| `GlassEffectContainer`                 | One blurred surface per group (tab bar, mode switch), not one per button       |
| Morphing                               | The active pill slides between tabs and between modes                          |
| Only on controls, toolbars and cards   | Not on headings, body text, the hero name, photos or Contact's notes           |
| No nested glass                        | A button inside a pane gets the edge and tint, not its own blur                |
| Readable text                          | 4.5:1 or better, checked over the brightest ray and the darkest water          |

## Decisions (proposed, please confirm)

- Real bending of the background is left out. Only Chromium supports it reliably, and the water behind is too smooth to show it. Glass here is blur, tint, a bright top edge and a soft shadow, the same in every browser.
- Glass is tinted icy blue. Plain white turned grey over the dark water in the mock-up.
- Phones use the same look without `backdrop-filter`. The blur hardly shows over smooth water, and it is the costly part.
- Solid panels under `prefers-reduced-transparency` and forced colours, and where `backdrop-filter` is missing.
- Contact's notes and polaroid stay as paper.
- `--butter` text becomes icy blue, and `--goldfish` becomes pale ice (the drip markers, pins and selection).

## Out of scope

- The background (spec 13), fonts, spacing, content, the angel, `og.jpg`, the favicon.

## Changes

- `src/styles/tokens.css`: dark tokens (`--abyss`, `--deep`, `--mid`, `--text`, `--text-dim`, `--ice`, `--ice-deep`, `--glow`, `--glass-*`). `--paper` stays, for the intro and Contact's notes; `--text` takes over as the text colour.
- `src/styles/glass.css` (new): the kit, its fallbacks and the phone version.
- `base.css`, `page.css`: move `.btn`, `.pane`, tabs, mode switch, top bar, fan, songs, drip and flow cards onto the kit; remove `--bleed`, `--goldfish` and `--butter`.
- `Tabs.tsx`, `ModeSwitch.tsx`: one sliding pill element each. Roles, keys and labels don't change.

## Tests (Vitest)

1. `src/styles/theme.test.ts`: computes contrast for each text and background token pair and fails below 4.5:1; fails if `--bleed`, `--butter` or `--goldfish` appears in `src/`.
2. `src/App.test.tsx`: tabs and the mode switch keep their roles and labels in the pre-render.

Pass checks as in spec 13. In the browser: every screen in both modes and Contact, at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844; hero button sizes with the drip label both ways; keyboard focus; reduced motion (pill jumps); reduced transparency.

## Risks and open questions

- Blur over moving rays is redrawn every frame on desktop. At most four blurred surfaces show on one screen; measure before and after.
- `page.css` is about 3,000 lines with colours written inline, so some will only be found by looking. Both modes get a full visual pass.
